# Dallas market notes (gathered 2026-11-24)
Sourced facts only; each is linked on /dallas-ev-data/.
- Texas: 532,282 EVs registered, Oct 2026, up from 456,667 in Feb (TxDOT).
- Texas: ~3,731 charging locations, 10,253 ports, avg public price $0.51/kWh (Axios Dallas, Aug 2024).
- City of Dallas plan: 1,500 charging ports for 39,000 vehicles by 2030, focus on apartments (DOE AFDC case study; Dallas Morning News).
- NCTCOG: $15M federal grant for up to 100 public ports in 16 counties (DFW Clean Cities).
- DFW Airport: 85,660,127 passengers in 2025, 2nd in North America (ACI via Airport Improvement).
- Cold weather: AP via NBC DFW (range loss, long lines); Green Mountain Energy winter guide; PlugShare survey of 580 Texas BEV drivers (74% had a gas vehicle); CleanTechnica on stations offline in Feb 2021.
Not verified (do not publish): response times, neighborhood charger counts. Served-city list (AREAS in src/lib/brand.ts) was carried over from the previous site and needs owner confirmation. Some third-party blog statistics on DFW EV counts were seen but not used because they could not be traced to a primary source.
